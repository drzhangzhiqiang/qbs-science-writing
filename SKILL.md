# QBS Science Writing

Use the book's reader-oriented approach: identify what a passage needs to do, arrange its information, and then choose language that conveys the intended meaning. Sophisticated writing depends on clear relationships and precise meaning, not ornate vocabulary.

This skill is an independent paraphrase, not the book or an official companion. Its writing guidance comes from the second edition. The routing and output format below translate that guidance into an AI workflow; they are not an author-prescribed algorithm.

## Choose the Appropriate Scope

### Revise a short passage

Identify the passage's function, clarify its connections and intended meaning, and then revise its language. Preserve the supplied facts and the distinction between observation and interpretation. Return the revised text, with a brief clarification only when a missing fact affects accuracy.

### Revise a section or manuscript

1. Establish the research question, intended readers and contribution from the supplied material.
2. Distinguish the activities performed, what the work achieved, and how that achievement contributes to the field.
3. Map paragraphs to communicative functions. Check whether the research question, findings and interpretation correspond.
4. Use the relevant section model as a flexible set of functions, not a compulsory template.
5. Adapt to supplied target articles where available. Without them, label the approach as generic rather than journal-specific.
6. Revise coherence, ownership, certainty and language. Check the title and abstract against the finished manuscript.

Do not impose a universal drafting order or require a collection of target papers before helping with a small revision.

### Reverse-engineer a target article

Label what each sentence or paragraph does for its readers, not merely its subject matter. Notice the sequence of functions, transitions and language choices. Extract a transferable pattern and apply it to the user's own material without copying the article's distinctive wording or findings.

The book recommends developing a collection of at least four recent target articles and updating writing models through reading. This is a learning practice, not a prerequisite for every request or proof of a universal journal rule.

## Writing Decisions

- Diagnose purpose and structure before replacing words.
- Connect known information to new information; make the relationship between adjacent sentences visible.
- Give paragraphs a principal purpose without imposing a fixed sentence count or one function per paragraph.
- Preserve technical terms when synonyms would change meaning or obscure continuity.
- Make clear whether an assertion belongs to the present study, another study, established background or an interpretation.
- Choose tense, voice and modality for meaning. Do not enforce all-past Results or universally prefer active voice.
- Results may contain necessary methodological orientation, explanation and bounded interpretation; they need not be a bare list of observations.
- Relate the Discussion to the question developed in the Introduction, rather than simply repeating the Results.
- Select the abstract's essential information for standalone understanding. Align the title with the completed work, not just its original topic.
- Treat the book's models and example lengths as descriptive guidance, not permanent publication requirements.

## Fidelity and Output

These are implementation safeguards: do not invent results, references or methodological details to complete a model. Keep numbers, citations and scope intact. Identify material ambiguity or missing information rather than silently filling it. Describe limitations transparently; do not use rhetorical revision to conceal their effect.

Follow the user's requested language and format. For a short edit, normally provide the revised passage. For a substantial revision, add a concise account of structural changes and unresolved questions. Do not produce a full audit when the user only needs a sentence.

Before returning, check: Does the passage fulfill its purpose? Can the reader follow its relationships? Is ownership clear? Is certainty appropriate? Has the rewrite changed any factual meaning?
