## Scope and Attribution

This is a selective synthesis of introductory methodology, structural models across the eight units, and relevant language and checklist passages. It is not a line-by-line study guide to every exercise, sample paper or appendix.

The substantive writing guidance is paraphrased from the source. The AI entrypoint, reference routing, concise response format and factual-preservation safeguards are implementation choices, not a separately named method in the book. QBS is the packaging approach used for this skill, not a term attributed to the author.

The book, scanned pages, extended quotations, exercise answers and vocabulary banks are not included. This independent project is not an official publication of, or endorsed by, the author or publisher. Consult a lawfully obtained copy for the full teaching sequence and examples.

Historical journal policies and factual generalisations in the source have not been independently verified as current requirements.
